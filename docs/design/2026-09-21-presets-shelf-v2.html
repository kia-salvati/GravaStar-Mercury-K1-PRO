Presets shelf v2 — the fixed shelf, and three designs that keep what works

Job 1 is the round-3 shelf re-audited through every interactive path and fixed (tab 0). Job 2 is three designs that keep the shelf's strength — presets as recognisable swatch-cards with Save current as a card in the same strip — and remove its 480 px weakness, where only three and a half cards showed and the detail card pushed the controls down. Apply now restores all three blocks on both links; the Link toggle is gone.

0 — Shelf v1.1, fixed

The round-3 shelf, kept as the baseline, with every bug from the audit fixed. Same structure: action card first, swatch-cards after, detail beneath, controls under that.

Found → fixed

  1. Strip jumped to the start after every action. Each render rebuilt the DOM and lost scrollLeft; selecting card 8 scrolled it out of view. → Scroll position is saved and restored on render, and the selected card is scrolled into view (inline: nearest).
  2. Keyboard focus was lost after every action and after every sheet. → Focus is restored to the same control after render; sheets move focus in on open and return it on close; Escape closes any sheet.
  3. Mouse wheel did nothing on the strip. Vertical wheel over a horizontal strip is a no-op in Chrome. → Wheel is translated to horizontal scroll while the strip can move; two chevron buttons give touch/keyboard users the same, and Left/Right/Home/End move focus between cards.
  4. No visible focus ring on cards, hover was the only affordance. → :focus-visible rings on cards, chips, squares and inputs.
  5. Stage blocks overflowed the 104 px card (speed steps were clipped). → Brightness and speed are stacked as two labelled rows of small blocks.
  6. Save current appended the new card at the far end, off-screen, with its rename input focused where you could not see it. → New presets are inserted right after the action card and scrolled into view.
  7. Replace mode hid every name behind "Replace?", so you could not tell which preset you were about to overwrite. → Names stay; a "Replace" badge is added to each card; the mode bar and the action card both cancel it.
  8. Escape during rename could still commit (blur fired after the render). → Rename commits only while the row is still in rename state; Escape clears that state first.
  9. "Preset N" could collide after deletes. → Default name is one above the highest existing "Preset N".
  10. The detail card pushed the controls down and back up on every select/deselect. → The detail area is always present with a fixed minimum height and a placeholder; nothing below it moves. (Still the shelf's cost at 480; designs A–C remove it.)
  11. Toasts never went away and stacked over the controls. → Auto-dismiss after 6 s, still dismissable.
  12. The notes claimed "at 900 all ten fit" — they did not (5½ visible in a 630 px panel). → At ≥ 700 px the strip wraps into rows instead of scrolling, so all eleven cards are visible.
  13. Sheet buttons had no Escape / focus trap; Tab could leave the sheet into the page. → Escape closes; focus is placed on the primary button and returned afterwards.

Cost that remains at 480

  • 3½ cards visible, the rest by wheel, chevrons or arrow keys; the detail area holds 196 px under the shelf whether or not a card is selected.

A — Shelf + live preview

The shelf stays; the detail card becomes a preview. Hovering or arrowing across cards shows that preset on a keyboard silhouette and in the ambient field without writing anything; the panel says "previewing — not written". Click selects; Apply commits; Escape reverts the preview and selection to what the keyboard reports.

Borrowed from

  • VS Code theme picker: arrow keys preview live, Enter commits, Escape restores the previous theme — the exact interaction model, including the "nothing is written until you commit" promise.
  • Lightroom presets: the preview happens on the subject (the photo), not on the preset tile — here on the keyboard silhouette and the field, so the tile can stay small.
  • Razer Synapse / SteelSeries GG: the device render above the profile tiles is the thing worth keeping; the sprawl of tiles is not, so the strip is capped at 10 and the render is fixed-height.

Cap

  • As the shelf: the action card turns into "10 of 10 — replace one" and enters replace mode with badges on every card.

Cost at 480

  • Still 3½ cards visible in the strip. The preview card is ~270 px tall and always present, so nothing jumps — but that is 270 px the controls card does not get. The silhouette is 84 keys at ~3.6 px pitch; single colours and per-key patterns read, subtle gradients do not. At 900 the strip wraps to two rows above the preview, so with ten presets the panel scrolls to reach the controls card.

B — Wrapping shelf + peek drawer

The strip wraps: at 480 all eleven cards fit in three rows of four (~330 px), cards shrink to swatch, name and two rows of stage blocks; at 900 it is six across, two rows. Selecting a card slides a drawer up over the controls card with the full readout and actions; the grid and the controls do not move. Escape, the scrim or the grip close it.

Borrowed from

  • macOS wallpaper / Dynamic Desktop: a grid of thumbnails of the actual thing, names secondary, the chosen one outlined — the swatch is the thumbnail here.
  • Elgato Stream Deck profiles: the current profile is marked in the list and switching is one action; editing lives in a separate surface — the drawer.
  • Lightroom: the presets list never scrolls the photo out of view; here the drawer overlays instead of pushing.

Cap

  • Visible by shape: eleven cells, the first being the action. At 10 the action cell reads "10 of 10 — replace" and every card gets a Replace badge.

Cost at 480

  • Cards are 84 px wide, names ellipsise at ~11 characters, and the effect name is dropped from the card (it is in the drawer). Three rows of cards is a wall of eleven similar boxes; the swatch has to carry recognition alone.

C — Swatch rail

A vertical strip of ten 38 px squares down the panel's left edge, mirroring the app's own rail: swatch only, the name as a tooltip on hover or focus, the current preset dotted. Beside it the preview — keyboard silhouette, readout, actions — and the controls card under that. It spends the window's 640 px of height instead of its 480 px of width, so all ten plus the action square are visible with no scrolling and no wrapping.

Borrowed from

  • Lightroom's filmstrip and macOS Dynamic Desktop: a column of thumbnails beside a large preview; you scan the column, you read the preview.
  • The app's own rail: at 900 the squares gain names the way the rail gains words — one component, two widths.
  • VS Code: Up/Down arrow through the squares previews live; Enter selects; Escape reverts.

Cap

  • The strip is exactly eleven squares tall (10 + action) and never longer; the count sits under it. At 10 the action square turns solid and reads "Full", and pressing it enters replace mode.

Cost at 480

  • Names are hidden until hover/focus, so first-time recognition rests on the swatch; two presets with the same colour and different stages look identical in the strip. The strip takes 48 px of the panel's width, leaving ~350 px for the preview and controls.
